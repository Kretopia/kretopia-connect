## 🚨 RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Waitlist Approval Bottleneck
**Problem:** Manual review = slow, users wait  
**Solution:** 
- Set SLA: 24hr max response
- Auto-approve high-quality profiles (LinkedIn/portfolio detected)
- Send "approved!" email with invite code immediately

### Risk 2: Diluted User Quality
**Problem:** Opening gates = spam/low-quality users  
**Solution:**
- Require LinkedIn/portfolio URL on waitlist
- Manual review first 200 users
- Community moderation later
- Flag/ban system

### Risk 3: Paid Users Feel No Difference
**Problem:** 3 paid users, but do they feel special?  
**Solution:**
- Add "Creator Pro" badge on profile
- Priority placement in discovery
- Weekly spotlight email
- Exclusive partner discounts

### Risk 4: Can't Scale Manual Review
**Problem:** 100+ waitlist signups = overwhelming  
**Solution:**
- Set expectation: "24-48hr review"
- Hire VA for $5/hr to review
- Eventually: AI moderation with human oversight
