## 🚨 Biggest Risks & Mitigation

### **Risk 1: Two-Sided Marketplace Problem**
- **Risk:** Need both opportunity posters AND creators
- **Mitigation:** Focus on creator-to-creator matching first. Opportunities are secondary.

### **Risk 2: Quality Control**
- **Risk:** Fake profiles, spam, low-quality users
- **Mitigation:** Manual review for beta, verification badges, invite-only launch

### **Risk 3: Feature Bloat**
- **Risk:** Trying to build too much too fast
- **Mitigation:** This roadmap. Ruthless prioritization.

### **Risk 4: No Differentiator**
- **Risk:** "It's just Tinder for creatives"
- **Mitigation:** Profile/EPK system + verified work history + AI matching = moat

### **Risk 5: Retention**
- **Risk:** Users sign up, never return
- **Mitigation:** Daily match suggestions, push notifications, membership benefits
