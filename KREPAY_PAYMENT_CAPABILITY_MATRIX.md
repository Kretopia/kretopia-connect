## Cross-cutting observations

- **Capabilities N and O (marketplace split / platform fee) are the strongest technical argument against replacing Stripe wholesale.** Any flow that currently takes a cut at the point of payment needs either a payment-facilitator-capable provider or a "collect gross, pay out net separately" redesign — the latter reintroduces capability J's regulatory weight.
- **Capability M (KYC/KYB) is the strongest argument for keeping *a* licensed provider relationship** regardless of what happens elsewhere — this is not something a from-scratch bank-transfer flow can responsibly attempt.
- **Capability W (chargeback/dispute) is a real, underexamined risk-exposure difference**, not just a feature gap — moving a card flow to SEPA changes who bears the loss from a disputed payment.
- **Capability F (reconciliation) is the one place today's system is honestly weaker than a naive reading might suggest** — the existing "manual payment" precedent is self-attestation, not independent verification. Any new SEPA flow should be honest about whether it improves on this (via a real provider webhook) or merely extends the same trust model to a new rail.
