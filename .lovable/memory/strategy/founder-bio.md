# Founder bio — Kretopia

## One-line
Founder of Kretopia — the Creative Operating System: an agentic AI layer that authenticates, organises and monetises creative work, born in Trinidad & Tobago, built for the global creator economy.

## Short bio (≤ 80 words)
Kretopia was founded in Trinidad & Tobago to fix the structural gaps Caribbean creatives hit every day: invisible credits, ghosted gigs, late payments, no operating layer. The product is now a Creative Operating System with always-on agents — Scout, Desk-Agent, Sponsor Radar, Pricing Co-Pilot — that authenticate work via the Creative Passport, find and draft opportunities, and run the back office. We are building the agentic IMDb + Slack + Stripe for the creator economy.

## Caribbean roots paragraph
Kretopia is Caribbean-rooted and globally deployable. The Trinidad creative economy — Carnival, music, film, fashion, design — is one of the most prolific cultural exporters per capita on earth, yet our creatives are systematically under-credited and under-paid. We started here because the problem is sharpest here, and because solving it at Caribbean scale forces us to build agentic systems that are bandwidth-efficient, language-aware, and cash-flow honest from day one. Every feature we ship for Port-of-Spain ships for Lagos, Kingston, Manila and Bogotá.

## Why agentic, why now
Creative work is non-linear, multi-stakeholder, and admin-heavy. The bottleneck isn't talent — it's the operational tax (chasing credits, drafting proposals, invoicing, vouching). LLM-powered agents are the first technology that can absorb that tax at the unit economics of a sole proprietor. Kretopia's agents already run on Lovable AI Gateway across Gemini Pro / 2.5 Flash / 2.5 Flash Lite, with a per-tool registry, daily caps, and tool-calling memory in `thrive_memory`.
