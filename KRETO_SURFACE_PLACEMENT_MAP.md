## Cross-cutting placement checks (all surfaces)

- No surface overlays a headline, primary CTA, form field, or piece of user content — every decorative instance is `pointer-events-none` or positioned outside content flow (Hero: absolute bottom-right, `pointer-events-none`; launcher: fixed, hidden while any dialog is open; `KretoTip`: its own bounded card).
- No surface renders more than one `KretoPresence` instance at a time.
- No surface uses a real user avatar, name, or private data as part of the visual (see `KRETO_PRIVACY_AND_TRUST_REPORT.md`).
